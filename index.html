About Me

Hello, my name is Errol. I am a student at Southern New Hampshire University, majoring in Computer Science with a minor in Software Engineering. I currently work with radio systems, and as my field becomes increasingly integrated with cybersecurity and technology-focused roles, I chose to pursue computer science to prepare for that transition.

When I began this program, I had limited experience in computing and was initially hesitant. With encouragement from my academic advisor and continued exposure to different areas of technology, I developed confidence and a strong interest in software development, secure systems, and problem-solving.

Through my coursework, I have gained experience in programming, debugging, software design, databases, and secure coding practices. This portfolio highlights selected projects that demonstrate my technical growth and readiness to contribute to real-world technology environments.
