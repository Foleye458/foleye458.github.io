About Me

I am a student at Southern New Hampshire University, majoring in Computer Science with a minor in Software Engineering. I have experience in secure software development, databases, and software design, with a focus on building reliable, efficient, and secure applications. I currently work with radio systems in my profession, and as the field continues to integrate with cybersecurity and technology-focused roles, I decided to expand my knowledge in computer science to better prepare for the future.

When I first began the program, I had limited experience in computer science and was hesitant about pursuing this path. With encouragement from my academic counselor, I chose to explore the field, and as I progressed through the coursework, my confidence grew significantly. Throughout the program, I have gained experience across multiple areas of computing, including software development, system maintenance, and secure coding practices.

Each course introduced new challenges and learning opportunities, helping me explore different specialties and better understand where my strengths and interests lie. Programming has been especially rewarding, strengthening my problem-solving skills and persistence. These experiences continue to shape my growth as a developing professional in computer science.
